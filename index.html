A LITTLE NOTE, KEPT CLOSE

A Note From Our Early Years

Dear Harika,

Sometimes, I think about how we started—as two little kids in primary school who had no idea how many years of memories lay ahead of us.

Back then, life was so simple. We didn't need big plans or special occasions to be happy. The smallest things could make an ordinary day unforgettable. We were just kids, growing up one school day at a time, without realizing that those little moments would become memories we'd carry with us for years.

If I could go back to one day from our primary school days, I honestly would. Not to change anything, but just to experience that little world once again. To see those younger versions of us, to laugh at the silly things we probably don't even remember properly anymore, and to enjoy a time when growing up felt so far away.

It's funny how time works. Back then, we were probably waiting to grow up, and now, a part of me wishes we could pause the clock for a little while. We didn't know how quickly everything would change, how much we'd grow, or how different life would look years later.

But one thing I love about childhood memories is that they don't really disappear. A familiar place, an old school memory, or even a random little thing can suddenly take you back. And for a moment, you're that kid again, living a day you didn't know you'd someday miss.

I'm glad our story began when it did. Not everyone gets to look back and remember someone who has been part of their story since they were little.

We've grown up, and there's still so much life waiting for us. But somewhere in all these years, those primary school days have kept their own special place.

So here's to the little kids we used to be, the memories that made those days special, and all the beautiful moments we haven't experienced yet.

If time ever gave us just one day back, I think I'd spend it smiling at how little we were and how much life was still waiting for us.

Happy Birthday, Harika!

I hope the girl you were back then would look at the person you've become today and smile proudly.

From your childhood partner-in-crime,
Always cheering for you.
